# Model use and governance gate

## Current status

**Status: prototype / human-reviewed decision support only.** The model is
not approved to automatically change prices, restrict service, deny an offer,
or determine any other customer outcome. A score is an estimate generated by
a model trained on one static dataset.

The project has no verified data licence or source provenance, no verified
prediction-time definition for `Churn`, and no observation timestamps. A
random holdout therefore measures performance on a related sample, not
performance on future customers. The missing `InternetService` value is
treated as the model's learned missing category; its business meaning still
needs source-owner confirmation.

## Evidence and limitations

- Holdout size: 1,407 records; positive-class prevalence: 26.6%.
- At threshold 0.50: precision 0.637, recall 0.564, F1 0.599, ROC-AUC 0.835,
  PR-AUC 0.625, and Brier score 0.140.
- The threshold is technical and provisional. It is not linked to an approved
  intervention, customer benefit, or false-positive/false-negative cost.
- There is no independent calibration study, temporal validation, external
  validation, confidence interval analysis, drift monitor, or live outcome
  feedback loop.
- Excluding `gender` and `SeniorCitizen` from model features does not prove
  fairness or remove proxy effects.
- The descriptive holdout review reports a 21.3% false-positive rate for
  `SeniorCitizen=1` (232 records) versus 10.1% for `SeniorCitizen=0`
  (1,175 records). This gap has no confidence interval or causal analysis and
  must be investigated before operational use.

## Release gate before real customer data or operational use

1. Record the data owner, source URL or agreement, licence, collection
   context, permitted purpose, retention limits, and an owner-approved label
   definition with a prediction timestamp and outcome horizon.
2. Identify every country where customers, the operator, and hosting or
   processing providers are located. Have qualified privacy/legal reviewers
   determine applicable rules and required notices, lawful processing basis,
   impact assessments, rights handling, retention, and cross-border controls.
   This repository does not certify legal compliance.
3. Use authentication, least-privilege access, secure transport, a retention
   and deletion policy, incident handling, and a privacy notice before
   accepting real customer information. The current public Streamlit app has
   no authentication or tenant isolation.
4. Define the as-of feature availability and prediction horizon. Use temporal
   train/validation/test partitions that prevent post-outcome information from
   entering features. Validate on later and, where possible, external data.
5. Evaluate calibration and uncertainty, overall and subgroup error rates,
   relevant decision costs, and stability. Set documented release thresholds
   with business and risk owners; do not tune on the final test set.
6. Keep a human accountable for customer actions. Explain the score's limits,
   provide a review/override route, monitor outcomes and drift, and prepare a
   rollback plan.
7. Review accessibility and usability with assistive technology and customer
   representatives. The UI improvements in this repo are not a formal
   accessibility certification.

Generate the current aggregate holdout review with
`python scripts/subgroup_audit.py`. It uses `gender` and `SeniorCitizen` only
for audit grouping, not as model features, and writes
`reports/subgroup_performance.csv`.

## Deployment controls currently present

- Runtime dependencies and Python/scikit-learn versions are pinned to the
  serialized artifact's environment.
- Startup checks the model schema, versions, and recorded artifact checksum
  before loading the trusted project artifact.
- Input validation rejects unknown categories, invalid numeric values, and
  contradictory service selections. Batch uploads are limited to 10 MB and
  10,000 rows.
- Numeric values outside the training development ranges are flagged in
  prediction results without being silently clipped or rewritten.
- Spreadsheet formula-like values are neutralized in the CSV download.
- CI runs unit, inference, and local Streamlit journey tests.

These controls reduce implementation and deployment errors; they do not
replace the release gate above or a jurisdiction-specific legal assessment.
